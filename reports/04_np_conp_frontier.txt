  THE NP vs co-NP QUESTION
  ═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  DEFINITION (Cook-Reckhow 1979):
    A proof system for TAUT is a poly-time computable surjection P: {0,1}* → TAUT.
    P is polynomially bounded iff every tautology τ has a proof π with |π| ≤ poly(|τ|).

  THEOREM (Cook-Reckhow 1979):
    NP = co-NP  ⟺  ∃ polynomially bounded proof system.
    STATUS: PROVEN (the equivalence, not the resolution).

  WHAT THIS MEANS:
    To prove NP = co-NP: exhibit a proof system P + show every tautology
    has a poly-size P-proof.  Candidate: Frege.  Problem: no one has proved it.

    To prove NP ≠ co-NP: for EVERY proof system P, exhibit a tautology family
    {τ_n} requiring super-polynomial P-proofs.  This requires proving
    Frege lower bounds — currently unknown and blocked by barriers.

  THE FREGE GAP (STATED WITH MAXIMUM PRECISION):

    We know (PROVEN, Haken 1985):
      ∀n: any resolution refutation of PHP_n has length ≥ 2^{n/20}

    We do NOT know (OPEN since Cook 1975):
      ∃ polynomial p: ∀n: ∃ Frege proof of PHP_n with length ≤ p(n)?

    If YES  → NP = co-NP  (extraordinary result)
    If NO   → NP ≠ co-NP  (expected but unproven)

    The gap between these outcomes IS the NP vs co-NP question,
    reduced to a single precisely stated question about the Frege system.

  THE THREE BARRIERS TO RESOLVING THE FREGE GAP:

    1. Relativization (Baker-Gill-Solovay 1975):
       Any proof technique that treats TMs as black-box oracle machines
       cannot resolve P vs NP or NP vs co-NP.
       Resolution, diagonalization: ALL relativize.
       A proof of NP≠co-NP via Frege lower bounds MUST be non-relativizing.

    2. Natural Proofs (Razborov-Rudich 1997):
       If secure PRGs exist: no "natural" circuit lower bound technique works.
       Natural = poly-time checkable + holds for ≥ 2^{-n^ε} fraction of functions.
       Most known techniques for proving lower bounds are "natural."
       A Frege lower bound MUST use non-natural methods.

    3. Algebrization (Aaronson-Wigderson 2009):
       Relativization + standard algebraic techniques cannot resolve NP vs co-NP.
       IP=PSPACE-style proofs use algebrization (works for containments, not separations).
       A Frege lower bound MUST be non-algebrizing.

    ALL THREE BARRIERS are PROVEN theorems (not conjectures).
    Any valid proof must simultaneously bypass all three.
    No currently known technique does this.

  WHAT A PROOF OF NP ≠ co-NP WOULD REQUIRE:
    Show: ∃ tautology family {τ_n} s.t. every Frege proof of τ_n has size ≥ 2^{n^ε}
    Method: Must be non-relativizing, non-natural, non-algebrizing
    Current best: AC0-Frege exponential lower bounds (Krajíček 1994)
    Gap: AC0-Frege → Frege = exactly the open frontier
    Progress: GCT (Mulmuley-Sohoni 2001) avoids both Natural Proofs and Algebrization
              but has produced no Frege lower bounds yet.
